The role

What you'll actually do

We're a US-based marketing company that generates homeowner leads for residential garage-floor coating contractors. Homeowners fill out a form asking for a quote. Your job is to call them fast, find out if they're a real buyer, and book an in-home estimate with our contractor partner.

This is inbound. You are not cold calling. Every person you dial raised their hand within the last few minutes. You're not closing deals — you book estimates that actually show up.

Your day

Responsibilities

  • Call every new lead within 2 minutes of their request
  • Qualify: homeowner, in service area, both decision-makers available, real timeline
  • Book the estimate onto the contractor's calendar
  • Run a confirmation sequence so the appointment actually happens
  • Work aged leads during slower periods
  • Log everything in GoHighLevel (our CRM)
