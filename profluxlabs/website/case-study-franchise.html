Before · The Franchise Problem

Lead gen for one location is a job. For twenty, it's a department.

A single shop can hustle its own leads. A franchise can't. Every territory needs its own ads, its own budget, its own follow-up — and every hour a lead sits untouched, it goes cold. He didn't need another dashboard. He needed the whole department, without hiring one.

  • ✕

    Ten territories, ten different ad markets.

    What fills the calendar in one state dies in another. Each market needs its own creative, budget, and kill-switch.

  • ✕

    Speed-to-lead, multiplied by ten calendars.

    A homeowner who asks for a quote expects a call in minutes. Nobody's franchisees are sitting by the phone refreshing a lead feed.

  • ✕

    Operators who install and close — not prospect.

    His owners are great on the driveway. Cold follow-up, double-dials, and confirmation texts were nobody's job.

  • ✕

    No way to see which market actually pays.

    Spend over here, bookings over there. Without one system per territory, the losing markets hide inside the winning ones.

The Deal · Pay-Per-Appointment
